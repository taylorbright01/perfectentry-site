PERFECT ENTRY / INFORMATION

Privacy & local data

Perfect View saves chart preferences, drawings and imported datasets in your browser on this device. The payout calculator also saves your entries locally. Clearing site data removes this local information. Export drawings before clearing data if you want to keep a copy.

Connected services

Public research and charts request data from the services configured for each tool, including Cloudflare-hosted feeds, Supabase and published research datasets. Those requests share ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, with the service handling them.

Membership purchases and billing take place on Whop. Community links open Discord. Each service has its own privacy information and account settings. Review those before using it.

Whop account features

Where Whop sign-in is enabled, it connects the website to the account and access service used for member tools and saved layouts. Account sessions are stored for that browser tab. Do not share login links, session details or credentials.

Questions

Contact the Perfect Entry team through the support links if you need help understanding the site’s data features.
