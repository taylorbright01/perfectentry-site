BEFORE YOU START

A few useful
answers.

What can I use for free?

Open Perfect View for charts, drawings and standard studies such as RSI and moving averages. You can also use the Session Screener, payout calculator and public research pages.

What does membership add?

Membership includes access to Perfect Entry’s own indicators, the signal dashboard, education and the member community. Check the current product listing on Whop for the price and exact inclusions.

Can I use this on my phone?

Yes. The site adapts to phone and desktop screens. Charts have touch controls and a mobile workspace panel. Wider research tables scroll within their own panels so you can inspect every column.

Are the charts and research the same thing?

No. Perfect View displays price data. The Session Screener displays research-based time windows. CurrencyAI pages track signal outcomes and system state. None of these establishes that a future trade will be profitable.

Do the tools place trades for me?

The website’s charts, screener and signal dashboard provide information for your analysis. Opening these pages does not connect a trading account or place an order.

Where do I manage my membership?

Use the Whop account linked to your purchase to manage billing and access. The terms shown on your purchase apply. If something looks wrong, contact the Perfect Entry team.

A question about your tools or access?

Reach the Perfect Entry team in Discord. For billing, use the Whop account linked to your membership. Please keep passwords and account credentials private.
