The person behind Perfect Entry

Taylor Bright

Founder of Perfect Entry. Writing about trading tools, systematic decision-making and how to question a trading result.

I founded Perfect Entry in 2020 to build tools that make a trading process easier to understand and follow. The website brings charting, indicators, CurrencyAI research and education into one place.

These guides explain the questions behind the tools: what a chart is showing, how a rule can be tested, what a performance figure leaves out and how to connect an idea to a repeatable routine.

What you can expect from these guides

Worked examples have explicit assumptions. Hypothetical arithmetic is kept separate from product results. External sources are linked where they support an explanation, and current product information is linked back to the relevant tool or research page.

A guide is a starting point for your own testing and judgement. It is not a personal recommendation or a promise that a method will be profitable.
