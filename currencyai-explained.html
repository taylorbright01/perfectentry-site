MEET CURRENCYAI

Give every signal
a second opinion.

A forex pair has two currencies. Your signal deserves that wider context.

CurrencyAI uses recent currency-level outcomes to assess incoming trade candidates. Signals that pass the system’s checks reach the approved feed. The rest are held back.

You get a more selective set of signals to review, with a record you can inspect before you make it part of your process.

FROM CANDIDATE TO CONTEXT

A clearer path
from signal to decision.

The value is in the selection. CurrencyAI gives you a structured way to follow the system’s approved candidates and see what happened afterwards.

01 / A CANDIDATE APPEARS

A setup enters the process.

The signal system identifies a potential trade. At this point, it is a candidate to assess.

02 / CONTEXT IS CHECKED

The currencies get a closer look.

CurrencyAI uses rolling currency-side outcomes and its rules to decide whether the candidate passes. Recent system behaviour becomes part of the selection.

03 / YOU REVIEW THE SIGNAL

The approved feed stays focused.

Passing candidates appear in the signal dashboard. Review the setup, choose your own risk and follow its recorded status through to the outcome.

LOOK BEFORE YOU COMMIT

You can inspect realised results, compare approved and rejected candidates, and explore published signal commitments. Build your view from the evidence.
