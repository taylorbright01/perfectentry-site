Using the tools & understanding risk

Perfect Entry provides educational software, research and information. The website does not give personalised financial advice. Opening a chart, screener or signal page does not place a trade.

Historical results

Backtests, recorded signal results and customer submissions describe different kinds of evidence. They may use different assumptions, fees, spreads or execution methods. They do not establish what you will achieve or guarantee future performance.

Your decisions

Trading involves risk, including loss of capital. Check price sources, times, broker specifications and your own account rules before relying on a tool. Feeds can be delayed or unavailable. Calculators are arithmetic models using the values you enter.

Membership and billing
